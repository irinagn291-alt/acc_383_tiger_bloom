Color 100 illustrated pages, including 70 tiger pages and 30 featuring other wildlife. Search by title or subtitle, browse by subject, and save your favorites. Pick up where you left off in My Art.

Fill outlined areas, paint with a brush, or erase color. Choose from 14 presets or pick a custom color. Adjust the brush and eraser size, turn on the option to stay inside the lines, and zoom in to work on details. Undo and redo edits, or reset a page and undo the reset if you change your mind.

In Photo Lab, choose a picture from your library or take one with your camera. Tiger Bloom turns the image into an outlined mosaic you can color in the same studio. Edits save as you work. Export a 1800 × 1800 PNG to Files or save it to Photos.

Tiger Bloom is available on iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later. The interface is in English.
