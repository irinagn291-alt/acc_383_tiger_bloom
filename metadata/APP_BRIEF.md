# Tiger Bloom

## What it is

Tiger Bloom is a digital coloring book for people who enjoy coloring tigers and other wildlife or making coloring pages from their own photos. Users can fill outlined shapes, draw freehand, revisit unfinished artwork, and export pages as PNGs.

## Launch and onboarding

1. The system launch screen gives way to “Explore” on every cold launch. There is no onboarding, sign-in, permission prompt, or welcome button to dismiss.
2. The header reads “Tiger Bloom” and “THE COLOR CLUB”. The bottom tabs are “Explore”, “My Art”, and “Photo Lab”.
3. On a fresh install, the featured card shows the uncolored “Poppy tiger” preview with “70 TIGERS. ENDLESS COLOR.”, “Let your colors run wild.”, “MADE TO BE YOURS”, and “Start something colorful”. Tapping anywhere on the card opens the “Poppy tiger” studio.
4. Below it, “Find your next favorite” shows “100 pages”, with “All 100” selected. The other filters are “Tigers”, “Wildlife”, and “Favorites”. The search placeholder is “Find an animal or a scene”. Any page card opens that page’s studio.
5. On later launches, the featured card shows the last page opened, or “Poppy tiger” if that page is gone. It shows “Keep your colors flowing” when the featured page has active edits and “Start something colorful” otherwise.

## Screens

### “Explore”

- The featured card opens the page shown on it, with its current artwork preview.
- Under “Find your next favorite”, “All 100” shows the full built-in collection. “Tigers” shows the 70 built-in pages with “tiger” in their title. “Wildlife” shows the other 30. “Favorites” shows favorited built-in pages.
- “Find an animal or a scene” searches titles and subtitles as the user types, within the selected filter, without regard to letter case. The icon labeled “Clear search” appears when the field contains text and empties the field. The result count displays “1 page” for one match or a count such as “0 pages” or “100 pages”.
- Each card shows a preview, title, and subtitle. The preview reflects saved edits and shows a heart on favorites. Tap the card to open the studio. Touch and hold it for “Add favorite” or “Remove favorite”.
- “Explore”, “My Art”, and “Photo Lab” open their respective tabs. Tapping a tab clears the search text. The “Explore” filter stays selected when switching tabs.

### “My Art”

- The header reads “Tiger Bloom” and “THE COLOR CLUB”. The gallery shows “YOUR PERSONAL COLLECTION”, “Look what you made.”, “Every stroke is saved. Come back whenever inspiration strikes.”, and “Your artwork”.
- The grid includes built-in pages with active edits and every imported photo page, including uncolored imports. Each card shows its current preview, title, and subtitle. Imported pages are named in sequence, starting with “Photo study 1”, with the subtitle “From your photos”.
- “Find an animal or a scene” searches gallery titles and subtitles. “Clear search” empties the field when text is present. The result count displays “1 page” for one match or a plural count such as “0 pages”.
- Tap a card to reopen its studio. Touch and hold a card for “Add favorite” or “Remove favorite”. Imported cards also offer “Delete photo page”. The confirmation reads “Delete this photo page and its artwork?” with “Delete photo page” and “Cancel”. Confirming removes the imported page, its artwork and edit history, and its favorite status from the app. It leaves the original photo in the photo library. A failed deletion shows “Tiger Bloom” with “This photo page could not be deleted. Please try again.” and “OK”.
- When the gallery is empty and the search field is blank, “Explore coloring pages” opens “Explore” with “All 100” selected. The three bottom tabs also remain available.

### “Photo Lab”

- The header reads “Tiger Bloom” and “THE COLOR CLUB”. The panel shows “FROM REAL LIFE TO COLOR”, “Your world. Your palette.”, “Make a photo page”, and “Turn a photo into a mosaic of outlined shapes, then fill it with your favorite colors.”
- “Photo library” opens the system photo picker for one image. “Camera” opens the system camera for a still photo on supported devices with access granted. Canceling either returns to “Photo Lab”. A successful import makes an outlined page, adds it to “My Art”, and opens it in the studio. “Loading photo” is the accessibility label of the progress indicator shown during processing.
- The instructions read “01”, “Pick a clear subject”, “Pets, plants and simple objects work beautifully.”, “02”, “Make it your own”, “Fill a shape or switch to the brush for little details.”, “03”, “Keep your creation”, and “Your page joins My Art. Export a PNG to Photos or Files.”
- Below the instructions is “Photos are processed on your device.” The three bottom tabs remain available.
- The alert title is “Camera and photos”. Its “OK” button dismisses it. When camera permission was denied, “Open Settings” also opens the app’s system settings. The possible messages are “A camera is unavailable on this device. Choose a picture from your photo library.”, “Camera access is turned off. You can still choose a picture from your photo library.”, “This photo could not be opened. Try another image.”, and “The photo could not be saved. Please try again.”

### Coloring studio

- The navigation title is the page’s exact title. The studio shows the built-in page subtitle in uppercase, or “YOUR PHOTO PAGE” for an import, alongside “Saved”. The system back control returns to the originating tab. The canvas has the accessibility label “Coloring canvas”.
- The heart is labeled “Add favorite” or “Remove favorite” and toggles the page’s favorite status. The icon labeled “Export” opens “Save PNG to Files” and “Save to Photos”. “Save PNG to Files” opens the system save dialog with the page title as the suggested filename. “Save to Photos” adds a PNG to Photos after permission is granted. Both export the artwork with its outlines, at full page, regardless of the current zoom. Artwork can be exported at any stage.
- “Fill” is selected on entry. Pick a color and tap an outlined shape to fill it. The hint reads “Pick a color, then tap a shape. Try Paper to clear a whole shape.”
- “Brush” draws with a finger. Its hint is “Draw with your finger. Turn off Stay inside the lines for freehand strokes.”
- “Erase” removes color where the user brushes and preserves the printed outlines. Its hint is “Brush over color to erase it. Your printed outlines stay in place.”
- “Move” enlarges the page and lets the user drag around it. Its hint is “Drag to explore the enlarged page. Choose Fill or Brush to keep coloring.”
- “Brush” and “Erase” show the “Brush size” slider and the “Stay inside the lines” toggle. The toggle is on by default. When on, a stroke stays within the shape where it started. When off, strokes can cross shapes.
- “THE COLOR BOX” contains swatches labeled for accessibility “Tangerine”, “Coral”, “Buttercup”, “Blush”, “Lilac”, “Blueberry”, “Sky”, “Lagoon”, “Leaf”, “Lime”, “Oat”, “Cocoa”, “Charcoal”, and “Paper”. The selected swatch has a checkmark. “Custom color” opens the system color picker without an opacity setting. Selecting a color while using “Erase” or “Move” switches the tool to “Fill”.
- The icons labeled “Undo” and “Redo” move one step backward or forward through the page’s edits. The count starts at “0 edits” and uses “1 edit” for one edit. “Zoom in” enlarges the page. “Fit page” restores it and switches from “Move” to “Fill” if needed.
- “Reset artwork” opens “Start this page again?” with the message “You can undo this while your edit history is saved.” “Clear artwork” clears the coloring, and “Cancel” dismisses the confirmation. Clearing is an edit that can be undone.
- “Export artwork” has “OK” and can show “Couldn’t create the PNG.”, “Couldn’t save the PNG.”, “Allow access in Settings or choose Save PNG to Files.”, “Your artwork was saved to Photos.”, or “Couldn’t save your artwork to Photos.”
- A failed artwork save shows “Tiger Bloom” with “Your changes could not be saved. Check the available space on this device.” and “OK”.

## Features

- “Explore” has 100 built-in coloring pages across “Tigers” and “Wildlife”, with search and a “Favorites” filter.
- The featured card reopens the last page viewed. Page previews show current coloring.
- “Fill”, “Brush”, and “Erase” support shape filling, finger drawing, and erasing with the outlines intact. “Paper” clears a whole shape when used with “Fill”.
- “THE COLOR BOX” has 14 swatches and “Custom color”. “Brush size” controls stroke width, and “Stay inside the lines” controls whether strokes can cross shape boundaries.
- “Move”, “Zoom in”, and “Fit page” let users work on details and return to the full page.
- “Undo”, “Redo”, and “Reset artwork” let users revise or restart a page.
- “Add favorite” and “Remove favorite” let users mark and unmark pages. “My Art” keeps edited built-in pages and imported photo pages together.
- “Photo Lab” creates an outlined coloring page from “Photo library” or “Camera”. “Delete photo page” removes an imported page and its artwork.
- “Export” offers “Save PNG to Files” and “Save to Photos”.

## Behaviours that can look like bugs

- An empty “My Art” shows “0 pages”, “Your gallery starts here”, and “Color a page or import a photo to make it your own.” Tap “Explore coloring pages” and make an edit, or import a photo through “Photo Lab”. Favoriting a blank built-in page alone leaves it out of “My Art”.
- An empty “Favorites” filter shows “Save the ones you love” and “Tap the heart in the studio to add a favorite.” Open a built-in page through “All 100”, “Tigers”, or “Wildlife” and tap “Add favorite” in the studio. A photo page’s heart can be toggled from its studio or card menu. The “Favorites” filter in “Explore” is limited to built-in pages.
- A search with no results shows “No matching pages” and “Try another animal, scene or a different collection.” Use “Clear search”, try a different term, or select a broader filter in “Explore”.
- “Undo” is disabled at “0 edits”. “Redo” is disabled until an edit has been undone. Making a new edit after “Undo” discards the steps that were available to redo. “Reset artwork” is disabled when the page has no active coloring edits.
- “Clear artwork” or undoing all coloring edits can remove a built-in page from “My Art”. Reopen it from “Explore” and use “Undo” after a clear or “Redo” after undoing all edits. Imported pages stay in “My Art” until deleted. The edit counter can still include a “Clear artwork” action even when the page looks blank, and “Saved” appears on blank pages too.
- With “Move” selected, dragging pans and tapping leaves the artwork unchanged. Select “Fill” or “Brush” to color again. Dragging with “Brush” or “Erase” makes a stroke, even when zoomed in. Use “Move” to pan. If a stroke stops at an outline, turn off “Stay inside the lines” to cross it.
- During “Loading photo”, “Photo library” and “Camera” are temporarily disabled. Wait for the studio to open or for “Camera and photos” to report an error. Use “Photo library” if the camera is unavailable or access is off. Use “Open Settings” when offered to restore camera access.
- Every successful import adds a new “Photo study” card, even when the same image is picked again. Deleting a photo page renumbers the remaining “Photo study” cards in import order.
- When Photos write access is unavailable, “Export artwork” shows “Allow access in Settings or choose Save PNG to Files.” Use “Save PNG to Files” or grant access in system settings.
- A cold launch returns to “Explore” with “All 100” selected and an empty search. Reopen unfinished work from the featured card, “My Art”, or its original card in “Explore”.

## Permissions

- **Camera.** Requested when the user first taps “Camera” in “Photo Lab” on a device with a camera and the system has yet to record a choice. Usage description: “Photograph an animal or another subject to make a coloring page.”
- **Add to Photos.** Requested on “Export” → “Save to Photos” when system authorization is needed. Usage description: “Save your finished coloring page to Photos.”
- “Photo library” uses the system image picker and grants access to the selected image without asking for broad photo-library access. “Save PNG to Files” uses the system save dialog.

## Absent

- Login or accounts
- In-app purchases
- Ads
- Analytics
- Account deletion flow
- App Tracking Transparency prompt

Users create artwork and photo pages privately on the device. The app has no public posting, shared feed, or community content.

## Data and support

Photos are processed on the device. The app keeps the generated photo pages, coloring edits, edit history, favorites, and studio preferences locally. It has no app-run uploads or cross-device sync. Exported copies go to Photos or the Files location the user chooses, which may be a synced location.

There is no in-app settings screen or on-screen contact or support control. “Open Settings” in the camera alert leads to the app’s system settings.

## Scanning and health

None.

## Platform

The app supports iPhone and iPad and requires iOS or iPadOS 17.0 or later. iPhone uses portrait orientation. iPad supports portrait, upside-down portrait, and both landscape orientations. The app uses a light appearance and presents English copy in every region. There are no region-specific screens or feature gates.
