Colour 100 illustrated pages, including 70 tigers and 30 pages featuring other wildlife. Search by title or subtitle, browse by subject and mark your favourites. Return to work in progress in My Art.

Fill outlined areas, paint with a brush or erase colour. Choose from 14 presets or pick a custom colour. Adjust the brush and eraser size, turn on the option to stay inside the lines, and zoom in for detail. Undo and redo edits, or reset the page and undo the reset if you change your mind.

In Photo Lab, choose a photo or take one with the camera. Tiger Bloom turns it into an outlined mosaic you can colour in the same studio. Edits save as you work, and you can export a 1800 × 1800 PNG to Files or save it to Photos.

Tiger Bloom is available on iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later. The interface is in English.
